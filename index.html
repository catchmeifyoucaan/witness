What this account cannot do

Losing the device, deleting the passkey, or switching authenticators strands the MON. There is no guardian and no seed. “Forget passkey record” only clears this browser. It does not delete the credential, and it does not move the funds.

The public key, hostname, and origin are fixed when the account is deployed. Moving from 127.0.0.1 to a real domain means a new passkey and a new account. Approve a transfer of the balance to that new account first. Nothing rotates in place.
